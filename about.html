A specialized studio built for commercial visual production.

Vexnaire Visual Studio is an independent visual production studio operated by Vivek Vala. We produce high-impact commercial artwork, key art, and master stills for games, entertainment, publishing, and luxury brands worldwide.

What we do

You share the project brief, references, and intended commercial use. The studio develops a focused visual direction, produces the artwork, reviews it through watermarked previews, and prepares the final verified masters in 4K or 8K.

The studio focuses exclusively on high-impact still visual artwork and master deliverables rather than video or animation.

Every project is directed personally from initial brief through to final full-resolution handoff, ensuring direct creative communication with zero agency bureaucracy.

Scope first

Resolution, revisions, file formats and usage terms are agreed before production begins.

Multi-stage optical inspection

Final masters are inspected at 100% zoom before delivery. We systematically check anatomy, edge definition, geometry, lighting continuity, and material fidelity.

Portfolio honesty

Showcase pieces are demonstration works. Client projects are shown only with explicit permission.

Direct principal access

Every client works directly with the studio director from brief through final master delivery. No account managers or communication dilution.

Directed by Vivek Vala

Vivek brings an engineering discipline to visual production: systematic prompt architecture, multi-pass detail synthesis, and strict pixel-level optical inspection at full resolution. That analytical rigor shapes how Vexnaire Visual Studio delivers clean, commercial-grade master files without typical generative artifacts.
