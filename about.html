A visual studio built to turn ideas into worlds.

Vexnaire is an independent visual design studio creating cinematic key art, luxury product imagery and campaign-ready commercial visuals for game studios, creative teams and premium brands worldwide.

What we do

You share the project brief, references, and intended commercial use. The studio develops a focused visual direction, produces the artwork, reviews it through watermarked previews, and prepares final delivery files at the resolution, formats, and rights confirmed in the written scope.

The studio’s current core offering is high-impact still visual artwork and master deliverables. Motion and video production are part of Vexnaire’s longer-term creative direction, but are not yet sold as a standard service.

Every project is directed personally from the initial brief through final approved handoff, ensuring direct creative communication throughout the project.

Scope first

Resolution, revisions, file formats and usage terms are agreed before production begins.

Multi-stage optical inspection

Final delivery is reviewed at the output size, file format, and crop ratios confirmed in the written scope. The review checks anatomy, edge definition, geometry, lighting continuity, and material fidelity.

Portfolio honesty

Showcase pieces are demonstration works. Client projects are shown only with explicit permission.

Direct principal access

Every client works directly with the studio director from brief through final delivery. No account managers or communication dilution.
