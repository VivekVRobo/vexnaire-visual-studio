Publishing, creator artwork and resolution-specific delivery remain available when they fit the project. They are retained as supporting capabilities rather than the studio's primary sales message.

Publishing Art Creator Artwork Resolution Options

Delivery Standards.

Resolution Tiers

4K is the standard delivery option. 8K and 16K are quoted only when the intended use, source artwork, and feasibility review support them; the written scope confirms the final resolution and format.

Final file formats

The written scope states the final file format. Web-optimized exports are included only when listed in that scope.

Crop ratios

Requested crop ratios are included only when listed in the written scope.

Commercial usage

Owned-channel digital usage can be included in the written scope. Paid advertising, packaging, outdoor media, merchandise, resale rights and expanded exclusivity are quoted separately.
