Delivery standards

Final files are prepared at the resolution, format, crop ratios, and usage rights stated in the written project scope. 4K is the standard delivery option; 8K and 16K are quoted only after a feasibility review. Standard owned-channel digital usage can be included in the scope; paid advertising, packaging, outdoor media, merchandise, and expanded rights are quoted separately.
