# Vexnaire Logo Usage Rules

## Approved versions
Use the official master symbol in:
- Ivory `#F4F0E8`
- Black/Obsidian `#0A0A0A`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Violet `#C9A6FF` as an accent variant

## Clear space
Maintain clear space around the mark equal to approximately the height of the lower central V point on all sides.

## Minimum size
- Digital symbol: 24 px recommended minimum.
- Print symbol: 8 mm recommended minimum.
- At favicon sizes below 24 px, use the simplified flat symbol only.

## Do
- Use ivory on obsidian as the primary expression.
- Use obsidian on ivory for light applications.
- Keep the symbol upright.
- Preserve the approved proportions and internal geometry.
- Use violet as a controlled accent, not as a mandatory effect.

## Don't
- Stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the mark.
- Add eyes, feathers, a bird head, or other literal falcon anatomy.
- Add unapproved gradients, neon RGB effects, or heavy gold.
- Place the logo on visually busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast.
- Recreate the symbol from scratch when an official asset is available.
