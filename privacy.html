Privacy

The site uses a simple email based inquiry process. The contact form prepares an email in your own email app.

Contact form

The form prepares the information you enter as an email. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the message through your email provider.

Email communication

Information you send by email may be kept for project communication, quoting, delivery, support and basic business records.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser information, request paths and security logs.

External services

Web fonts and other public assets may be loaded from external providers. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Payments

If a payment provider is used, payment details are handled by that provider rather than stored in this static website.

Client material

References, drafts and unreleased artwork are used for the project. Confidential work is not shown publicly without permission.
