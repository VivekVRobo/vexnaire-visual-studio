Quality Review

Final delivery is reviewed against the approved brief at the output size named in the written scope. Resolution and any requested crop ratios are confirmed before handoff.

The 4-Stage Quality Gate.

STAGE 01

Brief & Intelligence

We align on visual mood, narrative intent, target platforms, aspect ratios, and resolution requirements.

STAGE 02

Creative Direction

Our art directors build composition, lighting mood, color palette, and visual focal hierarchy.

STAGE 03

100% Crop QA Gate

Final-output review for composition, edge treatment, reflection detail, and texture fidelity at the agreed delivery size.

STAGE 04

Final Delivery

Final files, formats, crop ratios, commercial rights, and delivery resolution are supplied exactly as confirmed in the written scope. 4K is standard; 8K and 16K require feasibility review.
