Delivery Resolution Guide.

Portfolio work is presented as web preview imagery. For a commissioned project, the final output is reviewed against the approved brief at the delivery size confirmed in the written scope.

Quality Review

Final delivery is reviewed against the approved brief at the output size named in the written scope. Resolution and any requested crop ratios are confirmed before handoff.

Social & Web
1080p
1920 × 1080 px

Mobile storytelling, quick feeds, and video essay backdrops.

Standard Delivery
4K
3840 × 2160 px

The standard option for digital, web, presentation, and approved print delivery.

Optional Delivery
8K
7680 × 4320 px

Quoted only when the source artwork and intended use pass a feasibility review.

Custom Review
16K
15360 × 8640 px

Considered for appropriate projects after a custom feasibility review and explicit written scope.

Focused quality review

The final export is reviewed at the delivery size agreed for the project.

Material and lighting review

Material, lighting, and edge treatment are reviewed against the approved visual direction.

Scope-based exports

Requested crop ratios and web exports are included only when listed in the written scope.

The 4-Stage Quality Gate.

STAGE 01

Brief & Intelligence

We align on visual mood, narrative intent, target platforms, aspect ratios, and resolution requirements.

STAGE 02

Creative Direction

Our art directors build composition, lighting mood, color palette, and visual focal hierarchy.

STAGE 03

100% Crop QA Gate

Final-output review for composition, edge treatment, reflection detail, and texture fidelity at the agreed delivery size.

STAGE 04

Final Delivery

Final files, formats, crop ratios, commercial rights, and delivery resolution are supplied exactly as confirmed in the written scope. 4K is standard; 8K and 16K require feasibility review.
