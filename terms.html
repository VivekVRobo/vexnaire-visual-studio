Studio terms

The quote for each project defines the final scope. These terms explain the general working process.

Scope and deliverables

The quote lists the artwork, revisions, file sizes, formats, usage and expected timing. Work outside that scope may need a new quote.

Revisions

Included revisions cover reasonable changes within the approved direction. A major change of subject or concept may count as a new scope.

Payment and delivery

Payment timing is agreed before work begins. Preview files may be watermarked. Final files are delivered according to the agreed payment stage.

Usage rights

Commercial use can be included in the project agreement. Copyright transfer, exclusivity, merchandise rights and resale rights are only included when explicitly agreed.

Client supplied material

The client is responsible for having permission to use any logos, characters, trademarks, references or other protected material supplied for the project.

Production tools

Vexnaire may use generation tools, compositing, retouching, upscaling and other digital methods when useful. The final deliverable and its scope are what the client is purchasing.

Resolution

4K is the standard final-delivery option. 8K and 16K are available only when the artwork, intended use, and technical review support them, and only when the written project scope names them. A master is the final approved export at the agreed resolution; source files and editable layers are not included unless explicitly listed.

Portfolio display

Completed client work is only shown publicly when permission is available. Confidential or unreleased work is not published without approval.
