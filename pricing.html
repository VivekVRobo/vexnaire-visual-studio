Common questions

Key details on how we scope, price, and deliver commercial visual commissions.

How does project pricing work for custom commissions?

Every commission receives a confirmed flat quote based on visual complexity, deliverable count, target resolution, and commercial licensing scope with no surprise billable hours.

What resolution tier should I request for my project?

4K is ideal for web, social, and digital hero displays. 8K masters provide extreme detail for large-format print, physical packaging, and deep cropping flexibility. Ultra-scale 16K is available upon project evaluation for exhibition and billboard installations.

Can the artwork be licensed for commercial campaigns and products?

Yes. Commercial usage rights are scoped and included directly in your project quote before production begins.

What is included in the revision rounds?

Revisions cover palette adjustments, lighting shifts, material refinements, and compositional balancing within the agreed creative brief.

How long does typical production take from brief to delivery?

Single key art commissions generally take 3 to 5 business days. Multi-asset campaign sets typically deliver within 7 to 10 business days.

What final file formats and deliverables do you supply?

Deliverables include uncompressed master archives (TIFF/PNG) alongside web-optimized formats and requested crop ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).

Do you produce video editing or 3D motion animations?

Our studio currently focuses exclusively on still visual direction and ultra-high-resolution master rendering.

How do we initiate a project and lock in a timeline?

Submit your brief via our contact form or email. We review your requirements and return a confirmed scope, delivery timeline, and quote within 24 hours.

Not sure what you need?

Share the project details and budget range. The studio will recommend the option that fits best.
