02

Expanded commercial usage

What changes the quote

01

Creative complexity

Character count, environments, product detail, architectural fidelity, and substantial recomposition can materially change production effort.

02

Revision and schedule scope

Additional revision rounds, a new creative direction after approval, and rush scheduling are quoted outside the base package.

03

Resolution and deliverables

4K is standard. 8K and 16K require feasibility review and written scope confirmation. Substantial aspect-ratio recompositions and additional exports can also change the quote.

Common questions

How does project pricing work for custom commissions?

The packages above are public starting points. Every commission receives a confirmed flat quote based on visual complexity, deliverable count, target resolution, revision scope, schedule, and usage rights. The written scope is approved before production begins.

What is the Visual Direction Sprint for?

The Sprint is a direction-setting engagement, not a discounted finished commercial artwork. It is designed to test one composition or style route before committing to a full master visual. If a Signature Key Visual is commissioned within 14 days, ₹4,500 of the Sprint fee is credited toward that commission.

What does standard owned-channel digital usage mean?

When included in the written scope, it covers the client's own website, organic social channels, presentations, and launch materials. Paid advertising, packaging, outdoor media, merchandise, resale, and expanded exclusivity require separate licensing or scope.

What is included in the revision rounds?

Included rounds cover reasonable refinements within the approved direction, such as palette, lighting, material, and compositional adjustments. A new concept, subject, major recomposition, or additional deliverable is treated as expanded scope.

What resolution tier should I request?

4K is the standard final-delivery option. 8K and 16K are considered only when the intended use, source artwork, and technical review support them, and must be named in the written scope.

How long does production usually take?

A Signature Key Visual is generally planned for 5–7 business days and a Three-Visual Campaign Set for approximately 7–12 business days after the scope is confirmed. These are planning targets rather than guaranteed turnaround times; the proposal confirms the target date.

Are editable files or full copyright included?

No. The standard commission covers the final approved deliverables and the usage rights stated in the written scope. Editable/source files, full copyright assignment, perpetual worldwide exclusivity, and similar expanded rights are quoted separately when applicable.
